How to Play
  • Batting: Watch the ball and tap DEFEND, DRIVE or LOFT when the marker is in the green zone. Perfect timing = boundaries!
  • Shot risk: Defend is safe, Drive scores 4s, Loft hits 6s but mistimed lofts get caught.
  • Bowling: Pick a delivery, then tap BOWL when the meter is in the green for a perfect line & length. Don't repeat the same ball — the batsman reads it!
  • Keyboard: A / S / D (or 1 / 2 / 3) for shots · 1–5 to pick a delivery · Space to bowl.
